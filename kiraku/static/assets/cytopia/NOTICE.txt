The original PNG sprite sheets in this directory were copied verbatim from
Cytopia's repository under its GNU GPL version 3 licence. A complete copy of
that licence is included as LICENSE-GPL-3.0.txt. No warranty is provided.
The manifest records source paths, individual artist credits, frame metadata
and SHA-256 hashes. Original sheets are included as the editable asset source.

Kiraku's previews/ files are first-frame extractions of those original PNG
sheets, made on 9 November 2026. The source art's GPL licence is retained.
The city layout, renderer, maths engine and pupil interface are Kiraku code;
Cytopia's C++/SDL engine has not been imported or represented as a web port.

Featured artists: Kingtut 101, RockFort, Yusuf8a684, MaroonShaded, THEMAX,
and other Cytopia contributors named in the per-asset manifest.
